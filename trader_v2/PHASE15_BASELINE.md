## Frozen rule definitions

- **§1 sweep** — wick ≥ 0.10×ATR beyond PDH/PDL, Asia H/L or the 10-bar 5m pivot, close back inside. One sequence armed per sweep bar.
- **§4 pivot** — `ta.pivothigh/low(src, 3, 3)` semantics reproduced on the LTF as **≥ on the left, > on the right** (the first of a run of equal extremes is the pivot); verified 0 mismatches over 20,567 chart bars.
- **F1 CHOCH** — most recent confirmed opposing pivot whose pivot bar is after the sweep bar, re-evaluated forward; fires on a **close** beyond it; a wick does not fire it.
- **CHOCH retest** — the exact CHOCH price level, **zero tolerance**, no timeout of its own, no penetration rule, no re-arm; evaluated on LTF bars, must precede BOS.
- **F2 BOS** — most recent confirmed opposing pivot available after CHOCH, **CHOCH pivot excluded**; causal close through it.
- **§7 displacement** — the BOS candle itself must satisfy `range > 1.50 × ATR(5m)` **and** `close > low + 0.6×range` (long) / `close < low + 0.4×range` (short).
- **M5 timing** — governed by the existing `dispWait = 12` **chart bars** of the 5m sweep-engine chart; not a wall-clock timeout.
- **F4 FVG** — the three-candle FVG whose **middle candle is the displacement candle**; no such gap ⇒ setup invalid, no fallback.
- **§12 fill / entry** — entry is the FVG **far edge**; fill on `low <= E` (long) / `high >= E` (short), evaluated on 5m bars, `retBars = 24`.
- **§14 / F5 stop** — long `sweep low − 0.20 × ATR(5m)`, short `sweep high + 0.20 × ATR(5m)`.
- **§15 outcome** — +5R target, −1R stop, **adverse excursion checked first**, 144-bar timeout, $3.00 drag as `cR = costUSD/(r × pointvalue)`, evaluation begins the bar **after** the fill.
- **Architecture** — 5m chart, LTF layer via `request.security_lower_tf`; H1 = 1m and H2 = 3m are separate hypotheses and are never pooled.
- **Folds** — A `< 2026-07-16 00:00`, B `2026-07-16 → 2026-08-09`, C `2026-08-09 → 2026-08-31` (UTC).
